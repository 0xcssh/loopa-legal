Loopa — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

Summary: your cycle stays on your iPhone. Loopa only receives anonymous usage statistics and your subscription status.

Health data

The information you record in Loopa (ring insertion and removal dates, your schedule, bleeding, symptoms, mood, notes, and your answers during setup) is health data of the most private kind. It is stored only on your device (and its encrypted iCloud/local device backups, managed by iOS). It is never transmitted to us or to anyone, and it is never included in the statistics described below. Deleting the app deletes this data from your device.

What Loopa collects

Loopa has no user accounts and no advertising. From version 1.0.2 it uses two service providers, and nothing they receive identifies you or describes your cycle:

  • Anonymous usage statistics (TelemetryDeck). The app counts events such as "app opened", "setup step shown", "paywall shown", "purchase completed" or "a ring change was confirmed". These counts carry no name, no email, no advertising identifier, no dates and no health information, and they cannot be linked back to you. They help us see which parts of the app work. See TelemetryDeck's privacy policy.
  • Subscription status (RevenueCat). When you start a free trial or a subscription, the transaction details provided by Apple (product, dates, price, country of the App Store) are sent to RevenueCat under a random identifier generated by the app, so that we can count subscriptions. Loopa never sees your payment information. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

We do not sell this information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device by iOS. Their content and timing never leave your iPhone.

Subscriptions

Purchases and free trials are processed by Apple through your Apple Account.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the update will be published at this address with a new effective date. This version replaces the policy of July 13, 2026, which applied to versions 1.0 and 1.0.1: those versions collected no data at all.
